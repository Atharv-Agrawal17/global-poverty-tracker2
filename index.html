What Can We Do?

Poverty is a complex global issue. Helping reduce it requires education, employment opportunities, access to healthcare, infrastructure, social protection, and responsible community action.

📚 Education

Support access to quality education and learning opportunities.

💼 Employment

Support fair employment and opportunities for people to develop useful skills.

🏥 Basic Services

Support initiatives that improve access to healthcare, clean water and essential services.

🤝 Community Action

Volunteer, support responsible organizations and help communities in practical ways.

Data & Methodology

Primary source: World Bank Poverty and Inequality Platform (PIP)

Poverty line: $3.00 per person per day, 2021 PPP.

The latest available observation differs between countries. The application displays the actual observation year instead of assuming that every country has data for the same year.

Important: A later year can represent a World Bank estimate or nowcast rather than a household-survey observation. The app does not label every number as a survey result.
